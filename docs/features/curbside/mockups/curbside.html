Curbside Curbside

The block as the street itself, read from the curb. Every household gets a house-number plaque fused to a green street-name blade, status lives on real road signs (a yellow NEW diamond, an orange MOWER OUT work-zone sign, a no-passing pennant for a party), and recording is a stop sign. Near me is a stack of overhead exit signs. Day mode is sun-bleached concrete; night mode is asphalt under streetlights. Set in Overpass, the open cousin of the highway sign face.
