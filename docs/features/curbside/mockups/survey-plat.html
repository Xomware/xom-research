Curbside / Survey & plat

Your block as a recorded plat on drafting vellum. Every house is a parcel with a lot number, frontage and bearing. Households sit on a survey monument, and status is a rubber stamp: MET, NEW MOVE-IN, LENT MOWER. Light mode is vellum and blue ink; dark mode is a cyanotype blueprint. Typeset in Barlow Condensed (lettering-guide caps) and IBM Plex Mono (field book).
